# Course-Launch-Prompt-Library-

This prompt library is designed to support the marketing team at an education services company with launching online courses
- Business: Online education provider 
- Intended workflow: Support the marketing team in the development of course assets required for marketing promotion
- Problem to be solved: The marketing team are often needing to launch new courses in market on a compressed timeline and with sometimes limited or inconsistent information. The process to gather the correct information and create a product brief and associated marketing assets is a manual and time consuming process
- Automation potential: Each prompt response can support the following prompt or workflow - Eg, identify gaps in course info > questions for the university about missing info
- Risks and Limitations: Incorrect information, eg competitor info and over the top responses that need to be heavily edited
